ARRIVAL SYNC (PRO)
When one person taps "I left", Tween turns that one real drive into an arrival time — then tells everyone else when they need to leave to get there at the same moment. No more "on my way" guesswork, and an optional reminder nudges you when it's time to head out.

SAY WHAT YOU'RE IN THE MOOD FOR
Search in plain language — "we're bored, but not food" — and Tween turns it into places actually worth meeting at.

SAVE YOUR HOME
Join a meetup from a saved address instead of waiting on GPS. Your saved places stay on your device and are never shared in a message.

A SHORTER START
The first-run walkthrough is less than half as long. It shows you the one thing that matters — share where you are, see the fair spots, send one to your chat — and then gets out of the way.

ABOUT TWEEN PRO
Finding a fair place to meet is free and always will be. Mood search, saved places and favourites have moved into Pro alongside groups, planning and Arrival Sync.

ALSO
Once everyone agrees on a place, it stays agreed — a settled meetup could quietly come unstuck and send you back to "pick a spot". Fixed. And everyone in a meetup is named consistently, instead of showing up as "Friend" in one place and by name in another.
