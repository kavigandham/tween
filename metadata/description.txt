Stop arguing about where to meet.

Tween lives inside the chat. Open any chat, tap the + beside the text box, pick Tween, and share where you are. As soon as your friend does the same, Tween finds places between you and ranks them by how long each person actually has to travel — not by distance on a map, but by real drive time.

The fairest spot wins. Nobody gets stuck with the whole drive.


EVERYTHING HAPPENS IN THE CHAT

Your friend doesn't need the app. They see the spot, the map, and everyone's travel time right in the conversation, and can agree or suggest somewhere else with one tap. The whole negotiation stays where you were already talking.


FAIR MEANS FAIR

Tween compares real travel times from everyone's location and shows each person's minutes side by side. A spot that's ten minutes for you and forty for them isn't a compromise, and Tween says so.

• Coffee, food, gas, or a place to study — pick a category or search by name
• Everyone's travel time on every option, at a glance
• Agree in one tap, or counter with somewhere better
• Open in Apple Maps or Google Maps — your choice
• Set a max drive time so far-flung places stop crowding the list


NO ACCOUNT. NO SERVER. NO TRACKING.

There's nothing to sign up for. Tween has no backend at all — your location never touches a server we run, because we don't run one. Your friends, plans, and history stay on your device, and where you are travels only inside the iMessage conversations you choose. Place searches and travel times go to Apple Maps, the same as they would in Maps itself.

Location is requested only while you're using the app, never in the background.
